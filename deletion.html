BLIXO - Account and Data Deletion

BLIXO: Block Puzzle Game, by Proyass

How to request deletion of your account and data

To delete your account and the data associated with it, send an e-mail to proyass.apps@gmail.com from the e-mail address you wish to delete (or include your username so we can identify your account). Put "Account deletion" in the subject line.

We will process your request and delete your account within a reasonable time, and we will confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its identifier (including any linked Google sign-in).
  • Your username, profile picture (if set) and personal information.
  • Your scores, statistics, game results and leaderboard entries.
  • Your group memberships.

What is kept

  • Anonymised, aggregated statistics that cannot be linked back to you.
  • Records we are required to keep by law, for the required retention period.

On-device data

Data stored locally on your device (settings, saved game, cached scores) is removed when you use "Reset data" in the app's Settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions about deletion: proyass.apps@gmail.com.
