Privacy Policy for BLIXO

Last updated: 13 September 2026

BLIXO ("the app", "we", "us") is a block puzzle game published by Proyass. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and the choices you have. The app is playable as a guest without creating an account.

Who we are

  • Developer: Proyass
  • App: BLIXO: Block Puzzle Game (package com.proyass.blixo)
  • Contact: proyass.apps@gmail.com

Data we process

  • Account and progress: when you play, a pseudonymous account identifier is created (anonymous sign-in). We store your chosen username, best score, best combo, games played, lines cleared, play time, and, for the leaderboard and anti-cheat checks, game results (score, moves, a game seed and a moves log). If you create or join a group, we store the group name and membership.
  • Optional Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive basic profile information (name, e-mail, and profile picture) to link your account across devices. Your profile picture is shown on the leaderboard only if you enable that option in Settings.
  • Advertising: we show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. In regions where it is required (for example the EEA and the UK), a consent form is shown before personalised ads are used.
  • Crash and error diagnostics: we use Sentry to receive anonymised crash reports so we can fix problems. Text and images are masked, and reports are linked only to the pseudonymous account identifier, not to your name.
  • On-device storage: your settings, saved game and cached scores are stored locally on your device.

Why we process it

  • To run the game, save your progress and show leaderboards.
  • To keep scores fair (basic anti-cheat validation).
  • To display ads, which fund the free app (with your consent where required).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of the service and our legitimate interest for core features and anti-cheat.
  • Your consent for personalised advertising, which you can change at any time.

Third parties

We rely on the following providers, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google (AdMob and Google Sign-In) - policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data.
  • Supabase (authentication, database and hosting) - supabase.com/privacy.
  • Sentry (crash diagnostics) - sentry.io/privacy.

Your choices and rights

  • Play as a guest without signing in.
  • Change your advertising consent at any time from Settings (Privacy options), where available.
  • Reset all local data from Settings (Reset data).
  • Under the GDPR and similar laws you can request access, correction or deletion of your data, and object to or withdraw consent. To request deletion of your account and server-side data, e-mail proyass.apps@gmail.com and we will action it.

Data retention

We keep account and score data while your account exists. Local data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. When you request deletion, we remove your server-side data.

Children

BLIXO is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and international transfers

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Our providers may process data in countries outside your own, under their respective safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest version, and significant changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions or requests: proyass.apps@gmail.com.
